COLOR CODES
===========
Green frame:  ACCEPTED — ML found it, the case worker accepted it
Orange frame: REJECTED — ML found it, the case worker rejected it (false positive)
Red frame:    BY HAND  — the case worker added it (true negative / model miss)

DIRECTORIES
===========
false_positive/  — pages with at least one REJECTED box (orange)
true_negative/   — pages with at least one hand-placed box (red)

Note: a page with both FP and TN appears in both directories.
